Support

Tempo helps you plan and follow strength workouts on iPhone, even offline.

Report a problem

Email david.boidi.pro@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, the iOS version, the Tempo version shown in Information, the steps that led to the issue and what you expected. If it happens again, mention when it happens and how often.

A cropped screenshot can help if it contains no personal information. Avoid sending passwords, private contact details or your full workout history.

Rest alerts are silent

  1. Open Settings, then Notifications and Tempo to check notification and sound settings.
  2. If Tempo uses AlarmKit on your version of iOS, also check the alarm permission in Tempo’s settings when available.
  3. Check Focus and the volume, then start a short rest to test the alert.

A Live Activity is missing

In the iOS settings for Tempo, turn on Live Activities if the option is available. Start a workout and lock your iPhone. Dynamic Island is available on supported models.

Import, share or delete data

Copy the complete link for a Tempo routine and select Import in your workout list. To share your own routine, open it and choose Share. The link contains the routine data and recipients can import it.

Routines and workout history are stored on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes the local data for that installation. “Offload App” may keep it.
