Privacy policy

Updated November 19, 2026

Tempo works offline and has no account or ads. Your routines and workout history are stored on your iPhone. Usage statistics are optional.

Data on your iPhone

Tempo stores your routines, exercises, sets, reps, weights, durations, workout history, workout feedback and preferences on your iPhone. Tempo and its Live Activity extension share part of that storage on the same device. Tempo does not sync your workout history to a Tempo server.

With your permission, iOS can alert you when a rest ends using an alarm or local notification. A Live Activity can display workout information on the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island, depending on your iPhone and iOS version.

Importing and sharing

When you choose to import a routine, Tempo reads and decodes the link you copied to the clipboard. A sharing link contains the routine data. When sharing a routine or workout summary, you choose a destination in the iOS share sheet or copy the content to the clipboard.

Optional usage statistics

Usage statistics are off by default. If you opt in, Tempo sends events to TelemetryDeck GmbH to understand how the app is used and improve it. Events can include app opens, routine creation or editing, workout starts or finishes, timer use, and visits to the statistics screen. Some events include the number of exercises in a routine, how it was created, whether a rest timer was used, or a workout duration range.

Tempo does not send routine or exercise names, weights, reps, workout feedback, GPS coordinates, HealthKit data or contact details to TelemetryDeck. The SDK adds technical details such as the Tempo and iOS versions, iPhone model, configured language, region and time zone, display or accessibility settings, and measures of how often or how long the app is used.

The SDK uses a technical identifier hashed on the iPhone, usually based on the iOS identifier for vendor (IDFV). TelemetryDeck says it applies a second hash on the server. Events are sent over HTTPS. TelemetryDeck says it does not retain IP addresses on its analytics servers. Tempo uses these events for usage statistics and does not use them to track users across apps or websites for advertising.

See the TelemetryDeck privacy policy and its privacy FAQ.

Opting in and withdrawing consent

You can allow usage statistics in the initial prompt. To change your choice, open Information in Tempo and use “Share usage statistics.” Your choice does not affect workout features.

When you turn off usage statistics, Tempo stops creating new events, shuts down the SDK and clears the events still in its own queue. A transmission already underway may finish. The SDK’s own cache can retain pending events, including after offline use. They may be sent if you turn usage statistics back on.

Retention and deletion

Your workout data stays in Tempo’s local storage. To delete data from this installation on your iPhone, open Settings, General, iPhone Storage, then Tempo and choose “Delete App.” “Offload App” may keep the data. Copies you have shared and any iOS backups follow the settings of the service involved.

The TelemetryDeck FAQ says the provider does not guarantee a deletion schedule for archived events. Withdrawing consent or deleting Tempo does not trigger deletion of events already received. Tempo has no individual remote deletion feature. You can contact support about those events. Whether individual events can be located or deleted depends on the tools available to the provider.
